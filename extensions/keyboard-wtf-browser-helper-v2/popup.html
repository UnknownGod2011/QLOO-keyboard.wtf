Private by design

This helper reads or acts on a page only when you ask Jarvis to control a browser task. It communicates with the QLOO app on this computer. When AI page understanding is required, the app sends a bounded task snapshot to your configured Gemini browser model.

QLOO’s public privacy page has not yet been published.
