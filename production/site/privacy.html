Privacy

keyboard.wtf uses Google sign-in through Firebase Authentication. The service stores your Firebase user ID, email, display name, account status, server-authoritative allowance ledger, usage totals, payment and subscription status, and registered Jarvis devices or sessions.

AI and automation data

Content needed for an action you request—such as selected text, your instruction, a relevant screenshot or image, or task-relevant browser page content—may be sent from Jarvis to the authenticated keyboard.wtf backend and then to the configured Google Vertex AI model. Normal application logs exclude full prompts, selected content, browser content, screenshots, credentials, and message bodies. Compact usage metadata, model/pricing version, timestamps, outcome category, and accounting evidence are retained for billing, abuse prevention, reconciliation, and support.

Browser extension

When you explicitly ask Jarvis to control Chrome, the helper can access the current URL and title, tab state, visible page structure, controls, task-related page text, and screenshots needed for that task. The extension exchanges this information with the local Jarvis desktop app. Relevant task content may then be sent to the authenticated backend and AI provider only when required to complete the requested task. The official keyboard.wtf website may request a status-only handshake containing the helper version/identity and current connection readiness; that status response contains no URL, page title, page content, screenshot, account identity, or browsing history. The extension is not used for advertising, creditworthiness, or unrelated browsing profiles.

Payments

Razorpay handles checkout and payment credentials. keyboard.wtf stores provider order, payment, subscription, refund and event references, amounts, currency, ownership, and status. keyboard.wtf does not receive or store full card numbers, bank credentials, payment passwords, or one-time passwords.

Retention and controls

You can sign out, revoke Jarvis devices, disconnect or disable the Chrome helper, cancel a browser task, and request account deletion. Financial ledger, provider references, security audit records, refunds, disputes, and aggregate usage may be retained in pseudonymous form as required for accounting, fraud prevention, legal obligations, and reliable restoration. Operational records are protected with access controls, encryption in transit, managed cloud storage, backups, and server-side authorization.

Data is not sold, used for advertising, creditworthiness, or unrelated profiling, and is not transferred for purposes unrelated to the user-requested product function. Chrome extension data use follows the Chrome Web Store Limited Use requirements.

Contact support@keyboardwtf.com.

Public reviews

If you choose to publish a review, its rating, text, safe display name, and date are public. Your email address and Firebase user ID are not shown. One current review is stored per account; you may edit or delete your own review. Moderation actions are recorded in a private audit log.

Account deletion

A confirmed deletion request disables new account use while we verify subscription nonrenewal, remove operational account data and delete the sign-in identity. A pending request may be resumed to finish that same confirmed deletion. Required financial and audit records remain under a pseudonymous account identifier for accounting, fraud prevention and legal obligations; deletion does not erase those records or automatically refund past payments. If completion cannot be verified, we report the request as pending and provide retry or support guidance.

Optional Luma guidance

Luma’s caption tour works without a microphone. If you choose Voice and grant browser permission, pet-only microphone audio and conversation are sent to Google Gemini through a separate, short-lived Live session. Public website voice requires sign-in and a constrained token from our protected backend; permanent provider keys are not sent to browsers. Local Settings voice uses its separately configured Personal API key, not Managed credits. Only maintained product facts, the current help section and, in Settings, allowlisted active shortcut labels are guidance context—not account records, credentials, files, clipboard content, selections or screenshots.

Muting pauses microphone transmission and Luma playback while keeping the Live session connected. Stopping, hiding Luma or leaving its view closes its microphone and connection. keyboard.wtf does not persist Luma audio or transcripts. Optional public voice uses bounded admission counters and expiring leases for abuse prevention; retention cleanup must be verified before that service is enabled. Google’s processing follows its applicable service terms. Presentation preferences such as theme, reduced motion and tour dismissal are stored locally in your browser. Luma cannot change Settings or perform account or desktop actions.
